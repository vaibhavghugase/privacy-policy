Privacy Policy

Serpent Tower: Snakes & Ladders

Effective date: 21 November 2026 · Android package: com.sneckladders.game

In short: Serpent Tower is an offline game. It does not collect, send or share any personal information. It needs no special permissions and has no ads, analytics, accounts or in-app purchases. The only things it saves are your game settings and play statistics, and those stay on your device.

This policy explains how the Serpent Tower: Snakes & Ladders app (“the app”, “we”, “our”) handles information. It applies to the app as distributed on Google Play. The app is developed by {{DEVELOPER_NAME}}.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal or sensitive information. The app has no servers and never sends data from your device to us or to anyone else.

Specifically, the app does not:

  • ask you to create an account or sign in;
  • collect your name, email address, phone number or contacts;
  • access your location, camera, microphone, photos or files;
  • use advertising IDs, show ads, or include advertising or analytics SDKs;
  • offer in-app purchases or process payments;
  • connect to the internet. The release version requests no internet permission.

2. Information stored only on your device

So the game can remember your choices, it saves a small amount of data in the app’s private storage on your device (Android SharedPreferences):

DataPurpose
Game setup choices (number of bots, board size, rule switches, camera and castle-view settings)So your next match starts with the same options
Sound effects and music on/offTo remember your audio preference
Play statistics (games played, human wins, bot wins, ladders climbed, snake bites)To show your totals on the main menu

Player names you type on the setup screen are used only during that setup and match, and are not saved. None of this data leaves your device or is accessible to us. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in Android Settings → Apps → Serpent Tower → Storage, deletes all of it.

3. Permissions

The app requests no Android runtime permissions and no special permissions.

4. Sharing of information

Because we don’t collect any information, we don’t sell, rent, trade or share any information with third parties.

5. Google Play

The app is downloaded and updated through Google Play. Google may collect information, such as installation data, device information and, if you have allowed it, crash and diagnostic reports, under its own terms. That collection is done by Google, not by the app, and is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

6. Children’s privacy

Serpent Tower is a family-friendly board game suitable for all ages. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). The app has no ads, chat, social features, links to external sites or purchases.

7. Security

The data described in section 2 is kept in the app’s private storage, which Android protects from other apps. No data is sent over a network, so there is nothing to intercept in transit.

8. Your choices and rights

You can change your settings in the app at any time, and delete all locally stored data by clearing the app’s storage or uninstalling it. Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete on our side. If you have questions about your rights under laws such as the GDPR or CCPA, contact us using the details below.

9. Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes how information is handled, for example by adding online features, we will update this policy and its effective date before that version is released. Significant changes will also be noted in the app’s Google Play release notes.
